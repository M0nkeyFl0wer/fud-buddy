FUD Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2025

Last Updated: November 25, 2025

🎯 What Makes Us Different

FUD Buddy is designed to educate you about privacy. We intentionally track you to show you HOW much data apps collect, then we reveal it all to you. This is not a typical app—it's an educational tool about digital privacy.

1. What Data We Collect

📍 Location Data

  • Precise GPS Coordinates: When you grant location permission, we collect your exact latitude and longitude
  • City/State Information: We reverse-geocode your coordinates to get your city name
  • Location History: Every location you've searched from is stored locally in your browser

🖥️ Device Fingerprint

  • Browser Information: User agent, language settings, installed plugins
  • Hardware: Screen resolution, color depth, CPU cores, device memory
  • System: Operating system, platform, timezone
  • Capabilities: Touch support, cookies enabled, Do Not Track status

🎯 Usage Patterns

  • Preferences: Every food preference you select (no-meat, spicy, fancy, casual)
  • Search History: What you've searched for, when, and how often
  • Time Patterns: What time of day you use the app, day of the week
  • Behavioral Data: How many times you've used the app, which features

2. How We Use Your Data

Primary Purpose: Privacy Education

We collect this data to show you exactly what apps CAN track. After you use FUD Buddy a few times, we reveal everything we've collected about you in a "Privacy Check-in" modal.

Secondary Purpose: App Functionality

  • Finding restaurants near your location
  • Filtering results based on your preferences
  • Providing personalized recommendations

3. Where Your Data is Stored

All data is stored LOCALLY in your browser using localStorage.
  • ✅ We DO NOT send your data to external servers
  • ✅ We DO NOT sell your data to third parties
  • ✅ We DO NOT share your data with advertisers
  • ✅ Your data stays on YOUR device

4. Third-Party Services

Optional Analytics (Not Enabled by Default)

If configured by the site administrator, the following may be enabled:

  • Google Analytics: For aggregate usage statistics
  • Facebook Pixel: For demonstration purposes only

Beta Version Note: Analytics are currently disabled in the beta.

External Links

We link to privacy resources like:

  • Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF)
  • Privacy Badger
  • Surveillance Self-Defense

These external sites have their own privacy policies.

5. Your Rights

✅ You Have Full Control

  • View Your Data: Click "Privacy Check-in" to see everything we've collected
  • Delete Your Data: Use the "Delete All My Data" button to permanently erase everything
  • Decline Location: You can manually enter your city instead of granting GPS access
  • Clear Browser Data: Clear localStorage in your browser settings to remove all traces

6. Children's Privacy

FUD Buddy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you are under 13, please do not use this app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as we add features or improve the educational experience. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy or your data?

  • GitHub: Report an issue
  • Website: FUD Buddy is an open-source educational project

💡 Why This Matters

Most apps collect this data (and more) without being transparent about it. FUD Buddy shows you the reality of digital tracking. By experiencing it firsthand, you can make informed decisions about other apps you use.

Our goal: Empower you to protect your privacy online.

9. Legal Compliance

GDPR (European Users)

Your data is processed on the legal basis of consent (you grant location permission) and legitimate interest (educational purposes). You have the right to:

  • Access your data (view in Privacy Check-in)
  • Delete your data (Delete All button)
  • Data portability (export from localStorage)
  • Withdraw consent (stop using the app)

CCPA (California Users)

Since we don't sell data or send it to servers, most CCPA requirements don't apply. However, you can:

  • Know what data we collect (this policy + Privacy Check-in)
  • Delete your data (Delete All button)
  • Opt-out (simply don't grant permissions)
